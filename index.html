K-12 edtech company evidence of effectiveness and credibility of marketing claims

A proof of concept from Cobalt Collective. Each row rates one vendor on three separate measures: the strength of the evidence base, how closely its marketing claims match the research (one tile per claim), and demonstrated commitment to evidence gathering. The three are not combined into a score; they often point in different directions. Ratings describe claims, not vendors' intent, and come from public materials reviewed in November 2026. Vendor names are withheld; the point is the method, not the verdicts.

How to read the tiles. Each tile is one distinct claim found in the vendor's marketing, colored by how well it matches the evidence; outlined tiles are headline claims. Rows are ordered by the share of claims in line with the evidence or modestly stretching it. Vendors that make many specific, checkable claims (Vendor E: 47) have more claims that can be found overstated; vague claims are rated Not evaluable instead. Small claim counts (Vendor F: 8 evaluable) make shares fragile. Vendors A and F are provisional. Claim text is abbreviated, with product and place names removed.

Evaluation commitment, low to high: None (no outcome studies); In-house (at least one pre-post study or stronger, run and reported by vendor staff, limitations stated); Single (one rigorous outcome study with a comparison group, not necessarily independent); Repeated (at least two comparison-group studies, credibly reported, not necessarily independent); Sustained (multiple studies over time, at least two independent, more underway or announced, results published regardless of outcome). A commissioned study counts as independent when an outside organization ran it and the full report is public, including null results.

Badges. "ESSA ratings" are impact ratings; "(commissioned)" marks certificates the vendor paid for. "Other reviews" certify program design, content quality or state approval, not impact. Checked against Evidence for ESSA, the CASEL Program Guide, EdReports and vendor sites.
